How to Play Solitaire

  1. Turn cards from the stock pile and move them onto the tableau or foundations.
  2. Build tableau columns downward in alternating colors, from King down to Ace.
  3. Only a King may be placed in an empty tableau column.
  4. Build each foundation upward by suit, beginning with the Ace.
  5. Win by placing all 52 cards on the four foundation piles.
