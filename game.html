How to Play Go Fish

  1. You and the robot begin with seven cards.
  2. Ask for a rank that is already in your hand.
  3. If the robot has that rank, it gives all matching cards to you.
  4. If the robot does not have it, draw a card from the pond.
  5. Set aside a pair whenever you collect two cards of the same rank. The player with more pairs wins.
